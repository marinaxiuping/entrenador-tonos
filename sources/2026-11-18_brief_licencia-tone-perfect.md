## Description

The Tone Perfect collection includes the full catalog of monosyllabic sounds in Mandarin Chinese (410 in total) in all four tones (410 x 4 = 1,640). Spoken by six native Mandarin speakers (three female and three male), the collection is comprised of 9,840 audio files (6 sets of 1,640). The files below are available for bulk download, and can be explored interactively at the Tone Perfect site.

## Guide to Files

- samples: Randomly selected samples, 1 audio file for each of the 6 voices.
- tone_perfect_all_mp3.zip: All 9,840 mp3s from the collection.
- tone_perfect_all_xml.zip: Metadata for each audio recording, in two formats each (Dublin Core and a custom XML schema).
